Bar Count

Not connected to a Google Sheet yet. Paste your script link into config.js.

Tap a product, then speak the amount ("two and a quarter") or tap it in. Nothing moves on until you press Next. Blank means not counted; 0 means counted as zero. The product list comes from the owner's Google Sheet.

Settings

Enter once per phone. The PIN is set by whoever manages the Google Sheet.
